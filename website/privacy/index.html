Your data, explained

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 October 2026

In short

FAIR is an offline-first expense-sharing app. Expense records are stored in SQLite on your device and supported records can synchronize with Supabase when connected. If you scan a receipt, the selected image is sent to FAIR’s backend and forwarded to its configured OCR service for processing.

1. Information FAIR stores

FAIR uses local and cloud storage for different purposes.

  • Local storage: SQLite stores profile, group, member, receipt, item, assignment, debt, and settlement records on your device.
  • Cloud storage: Supabase provides account authentication and stores supported structured account, expense, and group records that the app synchronizes.

The receipt record stores extracted details used for expense management. The original receipt photograph is not stored as an image attachment in that receipt record.

2. Receipt scanning and external processing

When you choose to scan a receipt, FAIR sends the selected image over an encrypted network connection to its authenticated FastAPI backend. The backend validates the image and forwards it to the remote OCR service configured for FAIR. The OCR service processes the image and returns text, which the backend parses into candidate receipt items and prices.

The extracted information is returned to the app for review and use in the expense workflow. Receipt scanning requires an internet connection and availability of the relevant processing services. If scanning is unavailable or produces unsatisfactory results, you can enter receipt items manually.

External processing and retention: Receipt images are transmitted outside your device for processing. The retention, logging, and deletion practices of the external OCR provider depend on that provider’s policies and configuration. FAIR does not guarantee how long an external provider retains submitted data. Consider this before scanning receipts containing sensitive information.

3. Authentication and synchronization

FAIR uses Supabase Auth to support account authentication. When synchronization occurs, supported records are exchanged between local SQLite storage and the Supabase cloud database. These records may include profile information, groups, members, receipts, items, assignments, debts, and settlements.

Synchronization requires an internet connection and depends on service availability. The actual access permissions, retention behavior, and backup practices of cloud-stored data depend on the deployed service configuration. Do not assume that deleting local data automatically deletes corresponding cloud records.

4. Sharing expense summaries

Copy Split places a plain-text summary on your device’s clipboard. Share Split opens the operating system share sheet with that text. Depending on the information available, the summary may include the group, date, receipt title, items, totals, payer, and debts.

The summary is shared with another application or recipient only when you choose a destination. The receiving application or service has its own privacy practices. Take care when sharing summaries that may contain information about your purchases or other group members.

5. How information is used

FAIR uses information to organize groups and shared expenses, record receipt details and item assignments, calculate shares and balances, record settlement status, synchronize supported records, authenticate accounts, and process receipt images to extract candidate items and prices.

6. Data retention and deletion

FAIR stores expense records locally and may synchronize supported records with Supabase. Deleting local app data removes the local copy but does not, by itself, delete information already stored in the cloud.

FAIR provides in-app account and data deletion functionality. The information removed by a deletion request depends on the implemented deletion flow and the relationships configured in the deployed database. Cloud backups, service logs, and information processed by external providers may be subject to separate retention practices.

Use the in-app account deletion flow to initiate cloud-side deletion. Do not assume that uninstalling the app automatically deletes your account or remotely stored information.

7. Security

FAIR uses measures intended to protect information, including authenticated access to protected backend functionality and encrypted network connections. The effectiveness of these measures depends on the application implementation and service configuration. No application or method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

You should protect access to your device and account and avoid sharing sensitive expense information unnecessarily.

8. Third-party services

FAIR depends on Supabase for authentication and cloud data, the FAIR FastAPI backend for receipt processing, and a remote OCR provider configured by the backend. These services may process information as needed to provide their functionality. Their own terms, privacy policies, configurations, and retention practices may apply.

This policy should be updated when changes materially affect the information handled, processing purposes, or service providers.

9. Changes to this Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated to reflect changes to FAIR, its infrastructure, or applicable requirements. The revised version will be published on the FAIR website with an updated date. Where required, users will be notified of material changes or asked to provide renewed consent.
