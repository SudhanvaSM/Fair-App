Please review your split

FAIR helps organize shared expenses. It does not move money, verify payments, or decide what a group member legally owes.

Using FAIR

FAIR is an expense-sharing app for organizing groups, receipts, item assignments, balances, and recorded settlements. Use the app only where you are permitted to do so and provide accurate account information.

Receipt scans and calculated amounts

Receipt recognition is automated and can be incomplete or wrong. You are responsible for reviewing the extracted items, quantities, prices, tax, tips, rounding, assignments, and resulting totals before saving or sharing them.

Balances and settlements

FAIR calculates item shares and presents debts between group members. A settlement entry records that you marked a selected debt as paid. FAIR does not initiate a payment, confirm that money reached another person, or guarantee the underlying calculation reflects your group’s agreement.

Cloud sync and availability

FAIR uses on-device SQLite storage and Supabase cloud services for account and expense data. Sync depends on network access and service availability. Keep your own records where needed; do not treat the app as a financial ledger or backup guarantee.

Third-party services

Some features depend on Supabase, FAIR’s backend, and a remote OCR service. Those services may be temporarily unavailable and may have their own terms and privacy practices.
